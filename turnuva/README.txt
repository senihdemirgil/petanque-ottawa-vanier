PÉTANQUE OTTAWA-VANIER — SWISS + LIVE SCOREBOARD — V2

LATEST RULE CHANGES
- Swiss BYE is an automatic 13–7 win everywhere, including standings and the live public board.
- Knockout size is selectable: 4, 8, 16 or 32 teams.
- Two equal-size knockout brackets are created:
  1) Championship Playoff: top N teams after Swiss.
  2) Consolation Playoff: next N teams that missed the Championship bracket.
- Example: selecting 8 creates an 8-team Championship bracket (ranks 1–8) and an 8-team Consolation bracket (ranks 9–16). At least 16 total teams are required.
- Brackets use seeded placement and advance automatically through Round of 32 / Round of 16 / Quarterfinals / Semifinals / Final.
- Championship and Consolation brackets are visible on the QR/live spectator page.
